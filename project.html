Breakfast: Pancakes

    Ingredients

      1 ½ cups all-purpose flour 3 ½ teaspoons baking powder 1 ¼ cups milk 3 tablespoons butter, melted 1 large egg

    Instructions

    Scoop or pour the batter into the pan to take up about 80% of the pan. Cook until the batter on top has solidified into bubbles and flip.

    Toppings

    Enjoy with whatever condiments you prefer. Ex: Maple syrup, whipped cream, fruit, etc.

Lunch: Salmon and salad

    Ingredients

      Salmon: 10 to 12 oz cooked/baked salmon or 2 cans drained salmon Crunchy veggies: Chopped celery, red onion, and green onions Fresh herbs: Chopped fresh dill or parsley Dressing: Mayonnaise or Greek yogurt mixed with 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice and 1 tsp Dijon mustard

    Instructions

    Bake salmon at 400°F for 8–12 minutes until flaky, or use canned salmon. Whisk lemon juice, mustard, and mayo or yogurt in a bowl. Gently mix the flaked salmon, chopped vegetables, and herbs into the dressing. Enjoy on toasted bread, in lettuce wraps, or over a bed of mixed greens.

    Toppings

    Enjoy with whatever condiments you prefer. Ex: croutons, balsamic vinager, etc.

Dinner : Steak and mashed potatoes

    Ingredients

      4 medium potatoes (peeled and cubed) 6 tbsp butter 1/2 cup milk or cream 1 tsp salt, and 1/4 tsp black pepper 1 lb sirloin or ribeye steak (cut into 1-inch cubes) tbsp olive oil 3 cloves minced garlic 1/2 tsp salt 1/2 tsp black pepper

    Instructions

    Potatoes

    Place potato cubes in a pot of salted water. Bring to a boil and cook for 15 minutes until soft and easy to pierce with a fork. Drain the water. Add the butter, milk, salt, and pepper. Mash well until smooth and creamy. Cover to keep warm.

    Steak

    Dry the steak and season it with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Heat the olive oil in a skillet over high heat. Add the steak cubes in a single layer. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes without moving them so they get a nice crust, then stir and cook for 1 more minute. Turn down the heat to low. Add the butter and minced garlic to the pan. Stir the steak in the melted garlic butter for 30 seconds, then remove from heat.

    Toppings

    Enjoy with any condiments you wish. Ex: gravy, salad, etc.
