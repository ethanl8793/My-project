Breakfast

    Ingredients

      1 ½ cups all-purpose flour 3 ½ teaspoons baking powder 1 ¼ cups milk 3 tablespoons butter, melted 1 large egg

    Instructions

    Scoop or pour the batter into the pan to take up about 80% of the pan. Cook until the batter on top has solidified into bubbles and flip.

    Toppings

    Enjoy with whatever condiments you prefer. Ex: Maple syrup, whipped cream, fruit, etc.

    Ingredients

      Salmon: 10 to 12 oz cooked/baked salmon or 2 cans drained salmon Crunchy veggies: Chopped celery, red onion, and green onions Fresh herbs: Chopped fresh dill or parsley Dressing: Mayonnaise or Greek yogurt mixed with 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice and 1 tsp Dijon mustard

    Instructions

    Bake salmon at 400°F for 8–12 minutes until flaky, or use canned salmon. Whisk lemon juice, mustard, and mayo or yogurt in a bowl. Gently mix the flaked salmon, chopped vegetables, and herbs into the dressing. Enjoy on toasted bread, in lettuce wraps, or over a bed of mixed greens.

    Toppings

    Enjoy with whatever condiments you prefer. Ex: croutons, balsamic vinager, etc.
